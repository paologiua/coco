Pixel art sprite, 1024x1024, a single character centred in the frame, filling most of the canvas.

A cute budgerigar (parakeet) named Coco, seen FROM THE FRONT, facing the viewer directly, perched

gradients, NO soft shading, NO blur, NO glow, NO drop shadow. Large flat areas of a single colour.

Background: solid flat magenta #FF00FF filling the whole canvas. No ground line, no scenery, no
border, no text, no watermark.

- Chunky, rounded, cute proportions: big round head, plump body, short neck. Wider at the chest
